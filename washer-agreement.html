Washer agreement

Version 1, 30 October 2026

Parties

This agreement is between:

  • Sparqle (“we”, “us”), which operates sparqle.com.au, and
  • the washer named when they accept this agreement online (“you”).

It starts when you accept it and continues until either of us ends it under section 5.

In plain English

  • You run your own small washing business and use our website and app to find car wash jobs near you.
  • You choose which jobs to take, when to work, and whether to work at all. You can say no to any job.
  • For each job you keep a share of the price (see Schedule 1), all of your tips, and referral rewards.
  • You must work safely, treat customers’ cars and homes with care, and follow the rules in section 3.
  • If you’re 15 to 17, you need your parent or guardian’s written approval (Schedule 2) before your first job.

1. How it works

  1. Your own business. You are an independent contractor, not our employee, partner or agent. Nothing in this agreement gives us control over how you do the work, beyond the quality and safety standards in section 3.
  2. Choosing jobs. We offer jobs near you through the website or app. You decide which to accept. Turning down jobs, or not working for a while, never counts against you.
  3. Your hours. You set your own availability and can update it at any time. There are no minimum hours or rosters.
  4. Other work. You can work for anyone else, including other platforms or your own customers, at any time.
  5. How you do the work. You decide how to complete each wash, as long as you deliver the service the customer booked (see the service descriptions on our website) and follow section 3.
  6. Equipment and products. You supply your own equipment and products. We may offer a starter kit for sale or loan, but you don’t have to use it. Products must be waterless or low-water and safe for car paint and the environment.
  7. Levels. Your level (Standard, Premium or Pro) sets which jobs you can see and your pay rate (Schedule 1). We place you at a level based on your experience and ratings, and you can ask to move up at any time.
  8. Helpers and substitutes. Pro washers may send a substitute or use a helper. Substitutes must be approved by us first, be 18 or over, and pass the same identity checks. You’re responsible for paying them.
  9. Checks before your first job. Before your first job we check your identity and age. For washers aged 15 to 17 we also need the written approval in Schedule 2.

2. Pay

  1. Your share. For each completed job you earn 25% of the wash price the customer pays, after any plan discount. The booking fee is not included. Schedule 1 shows examples.
  2. Minimum job fee. Your share for any job is never less than the minimum job fee for that service in Schedule 1, even when the customer has a plan discount. If it would be lower, we top it up.
  3. Tips. You keep 100% of tips.
  4. Extras. If a customer approves an extra (for example, heavy pet hair), you earn the same percentage on it.
  5. Late cancellations. If a customer cancels within 12 hours of the job or isn’t there, you receive the full late-cancellation fee we collect.
  6. Referrals. You earn $20 when a washer you refer finishes their first job.
  7. When you’re paid. We pay you by bank transfer every week, within 7 days of the end of the week the job was completed. You get a statement for each payment.
  8. Prices. We set customer prices so they are consistent across the platform. We will tell you at least 30 days before any change that lowers your pay, and changes never apply to jobs you’ve already accepted.
  9. Tax and ABN. You’re responsible for your own tax. If you don’t give us an ABN, the law may require us to withhold tax from your pay. If you earn over the GST threshold, you must register for GST and tell us.
  10. Superannuation. You’re responsible for your own superannuation. If the law requires us to pay superannuation for you, we will, and we’ll tell you.
  11. Fines and deductions. We won’t deduct anything from your pay without your written agreement, except amounts the law requires.

3. Standards, safety and conduct

  1. Quality. Deliver what the customer booked. Take before and after photos of every car (never of people, number plates you can avoid, or house numbers).
  2. Respect. Be on time, polite and honest. Tell the customer before starting if their car needs more work than booked, and don’t do extra work they haven’t approved.
  3. Never enter a customer’s home. Work only in the driveway, carport or street parking space. Don’t accept food, drinks or invitations inside.
  4. Safe working. Stop work in lightning, extreme heat (over 35°C) or anything that feels unsafe. Take breaks, use sun protection, and use products as their labels direct.
  5. Water and drains. Use waterless or low-water products. Don’t let soapy or dirty water run into stormwater drains.
  6. Report incidents straight away. Tell us within 24 hours about any accident, injury, damage, complaint or anything that made you feel unsafe.
  7. No cash. All payments go through us. Never accept cash, other than tips.
  8. Customer details. Use customers’ details only for the job and never contact them outside the platform, other than through our messaging.

Washers aged 15 to 17

These extra rules apply until you turn 18:

  1. You need your parent or guardian’s written approval (Schedule 2) before your first job. They can withdraw it at any time by emailing us.
  2. Jobs are only between 8am and 6pm, and never during school hours on school days.
  3. Your parent or guardian can see the address and time of every job you accept. If you’re hurt or feel unsafe, stop work and contact them and us straight away. In an emergency, call 000 first.
  4. You must never enter a customer’s home, and we tell customers in advance that their washer may be under 18.
  5. We never publish your photo, full name, school or street.

4. Insurance, damage, privacy and photos

  1. Our insurance. We will keep public liability insurance of at least $10 million. It’s intended to cover damage to customers’ cars and property while you are doing a job booked through us.
  2. Pro washers. If you’re at Pro level, you must also hold your own public liability insurance of at least $5 million and send us a copy each year.
  3. If something is damaged. Report it straight away with photos. We handle the claim with the customer. You won’t be asked to pay for accidental damage. Washers aged 15 to 17 are never asked to contribute. If an adult washer causes damage deliberately, we may ask them to contribute up to the amount of our insurance excess, but only if they agree in writing after the incident, and it’s never deducted from pay.
  4. Your own safety. You’re responsible for your own health and safety while working, and we’ll work with you to keep jobs safe.
  5. Privacy. We handle your personal information as set out in our Privacy Policy. You must keep customers’ information private and delete job details from your phone once a job is finished and paid.
  6. Photos. You give us permission to use your before and after car photos on our website and social media. We never show people, number plates or house numbers, or anything that identifies a washer under 18.

5. Ending the agreement, disputes and general terms

  1. You can stop any time. Just tell us. We pay you for all completed jobs.
  2. When we can end it. We can end this agreement or pause your account by giving you 14 days’ written notice, or immediately if you seriously break it (for example, dishonesty, unsafe conduct, harming a customer, or taking cash payments). We’ll tell you the reason in writing.
  3. Review. Before we end the agreement for a reason other than serious misconduct, we’ll tell you what’s wrong, give you a fair chance to respond, and consider what you say.
  4. Disputes. If we disagree, we’ll first try to sort it out by talking, within 14 days. If that doesn’t work, either of us can ask for mediation, for example through the NSW Small Business Commissioner, before going to court.
  5. Changes to this agreement. We’ll give you at least 30 days’ written notice of any change, and changes never apply to jobs you’ve already accepted. If you don’t agree, you can end the agreement before the change takes effect.
  6. No restraint. After this agreement ends you’re free to wash cars for anyone, including customers you met through us, but you must not use our customer list or contact details to do it.
  7. Notices. Notices are given by email to the address in your account, or to us at enquire@sparqle.com.au.
  8. Whole agreement. This agreement, its schedules and our Privacy Policy are the whole agreement between us. If any part is invalid, the rest still applies.
  9. Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales.
  10. Accepting online. You accept this agreement by ticking “I agree” in your account. We keep a dated record and send you a copy by email.

Schedule 2: Parent or guardian consent (washers aged 15 to 17)

This is completed online through the approval link we email to the parent or guardian. We keep the signed record. The parent or guardian confirms that:

  1. They’re the parent or legal guardian of the washer.
  2. They’ve read this washer agreement, including the extra rules for washers aged 15 to 17 in section 3.
  3. They’re happy for the washer to accept car wash jobs through sparqle.com.au, on the terms of this agreement.
  4. They can see the address and time of every job the washer accepts, and can withdraw consent at any time by emailing enquire@sparqle.com.au.
  5. In an emergency, we can contact them on their mobile number.
