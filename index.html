    Questions

    Who will wash my car?

    A washer who lives nearby. Every washer is ID-checked and rated by customers. You choose the level: Standard, Premium or Pro.

    Do I need to provide water or power?

    No. Washers bring waterless products and their own equipment.

    How do I pay?

    You pay in the app when you book. Washers never take cash, and tips are optional.

    What if my car is extra dirty?

    Heavy pet hair, sand or mould can take longer. Your washer will suggest an extra in the app, and nothing is charged unless you approve it.

    Can I skip or cancel a plan?

    Yes. Skip a wash or cancel your plan any time in the app.

    Book your first wash
