    About us

    We started this because the small, boring chores, like washing the car, eat into the weekends we’d rather spend together. So we built a simpler way: a neighbour you can trust, a few taps on your phone, and a clean car in your driveway while you get on with your day.

    It works both ways. For the people doing the washing, it’s honest work on their own street, on their own schedule, paid properly. We live here, we wash here, and we’re the ones who answer your messages.

    Questions

    Who can sign up?

    Anyone. Whether you want a flexible side hustle that fits around school, uni, work or family, or you run a detailing business and want more customers, you can sign up and pick jobs near you.

    What do I need?

    Waterless car wash products and basic equipment like microfibre cloths. Experienced washers and Pro detailers bring their own professional gear.

    How much can I earn?

    Up to $150 per wash, excluding tips. What you earn depends on the job, the car’s size and your level.

    What happens after I sign up?

    Questions

    Who will wash my car?

    A washer who lives nearby. Every washer is ID-checked and rated by customers. You choose the level: Standard, Premium or Pro.

    Do I need to provide water or power?

    No. Washers bring waterless products and their own equipment.

    How do I pay?

    You pay in the app when you book. Washers never take cash, and tips are optional.

    What if my car is extra dirty?

    Heavy pet hair, sand or mould can take longer. Your washer will suggest an extra in the app, and nothing is charged unless you approve it.

    Can I skip or cancel a plan?

    Yes. Skip a wash or cancel your plan any time in the app.
